# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Tech workers and high-income professionals who have money spread across banks, brokerages, retirement accounts, equity compensation, real estate, and debt. They want one current, credible picture of gross and estimated after-tax net worth without maintaining a spreadsheet.

## Product Purpose

Basis connects financial institutions, preserves institution-provided cost basis when available, makes missing data explicit, and turns scattered balances into an honest net-worth view. Success means a user can connect accounts, understand how fresh and complete the data is, and trust every number enough to make planning decisions.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, savage, and playful. The user explicitly opts into being roasted, so Basis may roast them directly along with their concentration, stale connections, missing basis, spending, debt, and dubious assumptions. The voice should feel like a financially literate friend who brought receipts, not a compliance department that discovered memes. High-stakes explanations remain plain, accurate, and calm beneath the jokes.

## Anti-references

Basis should not feel like a stuffy bank portal, a patronizing financial-literacy course, a casino-style trading app, or a meme feed wearing a budget dashboard. Avoid cruelty about protected traits or circumstances outside the user's control, panic, fake urgency, unexplained precision, and jokes that obscure actions or errors. Never fabricate peer comparisons, percentiles, or benchmark data for a punchline.

## Design Principles

1. Roast the user and the number, with explicit consent and actual receipts.
2. Show the source, freshness, and completeness behind every important total.
3. Prefer an honest unknown over a confident fiction.
4. Keep institution connection and repair flows familiar and reversible.
5. Earn every joke with a useful diagnosis or next step immediately afterward.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Do not communicate financial state through color alone. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, clear error text, and direct language. Humor must remain understandable when icons, color, or surrounding context are unavailable.
